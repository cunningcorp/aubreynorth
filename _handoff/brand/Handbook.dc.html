Register

Sharp and witty — but dry, never zany.

Warm and whimsical, but never too poetic — copy must always paint a clear picture of what we do as a business.

The rules
·Wit is a scalpel: one clever turn per piece, maximum.
·Confidence, no hype — no exclamation marks, no "revolutionary".
·The 1/8th principle — show the one lit idea; never list every venture.
·Story leads, method follows.
·Volume per division: Ideas loudest, Publications & Productions measured, Aubrey North quietest.
